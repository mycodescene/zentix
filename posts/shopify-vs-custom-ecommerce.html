Shopify vs custom e-commerce: which is right for your store?

2026-10-23 · 7 min read

Every store owner eventually faces this question: build on Shopify, or build something custom? The honest answer is that both are right, for different stores. Here's how to tell which one is yours.

Choose Shopify when speed matters

Shopify gets you selling in days, not months. Payments, hosting, security, and mobile checkout all work out of the box. For most new stores doing under seven figures a year, Shopify is the rational choice. The monthly cost is predictable, and the app ecosystem covers almost every common need.

Choose custom when Shopify fights you

Shopify starts to strain when your business model doesn't fit its mold: complex B2B pricing, unusual checkout flows, deep integrations with warehouse or ERP systems, or markets where you need full control over performance and data. At that point, the workarounds cost more than building it right.

The middle path most stores actually need

Here's what agencies rarely tell you: most stores don't need to choose. A well-built Shopify store with custom theme work and the right apps covers 90 percent of businesses. The expensive rebuilds happen when stores outgrow a sloppy initial setup, not when they outgrow Shopify itself.

What it costs

A professional Shopify setup with a custom-feeling theme runs a fraction of a custom build. A fully custom store is a serious investment, justified only when the business case is clear. Anyone quoting you a custom build without asking about your order volume and margins is selling, not advising.

The bottom line

Start with the business, not the technology. If Shopify fits, use it well. If it doesn't, build custom once and build it right. Either way, the store should pay for itself in months, not years.
